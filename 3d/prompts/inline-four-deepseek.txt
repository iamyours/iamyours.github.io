Build me a car engine in interactive 3D, from air intake to exhaust, in one HTML file with Three.js.
Make it look real: an inline-four with the air filter and throttle body, the intake manifold, the cylinder head with camshafts and valves, the engine block, four pistons on connecting rods, the crankshaft, the flywheel, the timing belt, the oil pan, and the exhaust manifold. Each spark plug fires a glow in its cylinder in the right firing order. The crankshaft turns, the pistons move up and down in step with it, the camshafts open and close the valves at the right times, and the timing belt runs. I can drag to orbit around it and scroll to zoom.
Add an Explode button. When I press it, every part slides apart to its own spot with its name on a label: the head and cams go up, the oil pan drops down, the manifolds move out to the sides, and the pistons and crank pull out of the block. Press it again and it all snaps back together. When I hover a part, it lights up and shows one line on what it does.
No images. Do not ask me any questions. Just build it.

【通用技术要求】
- 直接输出一个可在现代浏览器打开的完整单文件 HTML 代码（一个代码块，从 <!DOCTYPE html> 到 </html>，不要省略任何部分）。页面语言 zh-CN，标题用中文。
- 使用 Three.js r160，通过 importmap 从 CDN 固定版本加载："three": "https://unpkg.com/three@0.160.0/build/three.module.js"，"three/addons/": "https://unpkg.com/three@0.160.0/examples/jsm/"。除此之外不引用任何外部资源。
- 所有几何体、材质、贴图都用代码程序化生成（可用 Canvas/Shader 生成贴图），不要加载 glb/gltf/obj/fbx、图片或 SVG。
- 使用 OrbitControls 支持拖动旋转、滚轮/双指缩放（第一人称玩法的场景除外，按场景说明）。
- 画布全屏、响应式适配窗口大小变化和手机屏幕，devicePixelRatio 最多取 2，目标稳定 60FPS。
- 页面左上角放一个固定的链接「← 返回 3D 作品」，href="../"，半透明奶油色圆角小按钮样式。
- 在 </body> 前加上这一行（原样保留，用于展示提示词）：<script src="../prompt-panel.js" data-id="inline-four-deepseek"></script>
- 控制台不能有报错。不要问我任何问题，直接构建。
