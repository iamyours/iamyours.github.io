How it's drawn
  1. An orthographic camera (31° up, 45° + 90° steps) renders the scene into a tiny target, about 480×300, with no anti-aliasing. The camera is snapped so the world origin lands on a whole pixel, so static things never shimmer.
  2. The ice samples a second target holding the scene mirrored about the ice plane. Gloss rises on wet, fresh ice and drops where it is scratched.
  3. A post pass darkens pixels that stand more than 0.55 m in front of a neighbour (depth outlines), then maps every pixel to the nearest of 49 winter colours in OKLab, with optional 4×4 Bayer dither.
  4. The result is upscaled with texelFetch so every art pixel is an exact block of device pixels. Background pixels show the page colour.
  5. Skaters are ~16 boxes placed every frame with two-bone IK for legs and arms. Their blades write scratches into a 20 px/m wear texture that the resurfacer wipes clean.
